FAQ

Sawal jo har banda poochta hai.

If you can't read my files, how do you store them?

Encryption happens on YOUR device before anything leaves it. Our servers only ever receive meaningless random-looking fragments. Your password never reaches us — so we literally cannot peek.

What if I forget my password?

You get a one-time Recovery Key at signup. Password + Recovery Key both lost = data unrecoverable by anyone, including us. We warn you loudly about this because we take zero-knowledge seriously.

Which platforms are supported?

Windows app first (beta). Mac/Linux versions are on the roadmap.

Is my data really safe from hackers?

Every fragment is independently AES-256-GCM encrypted with keys derived on your device. Even a full server breach yields only random bytes with maximum entropy — mathematically useless without your key.

When will Pro be available?

Pro is actively being developed. Features include cross-device restore, split-file encryption, file versioning and more. Stay tuned!
